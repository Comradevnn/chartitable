How your gift works

Farms grow more than stores will buy. We close the gap, and we show our work.

  1. We pick it up

    Farms tell us when they have surplus. Drivers collect it and bring it to the warehouse the same week.

  2. Pantries serve it

    Staff log every update as it happens: meals served, pounds of produce received. Those logs are the facts.

  3. You see what it did

    After you give, you get a page with the logged facts and your estimated share, each clearly labeled.

More ways to help

  • Volunteer

    Sort produce at the warehouse. Shifts are short and no experience is needed.

  • Start a fundraiser

    A birthday, a race or an office drive. We will set up a page for it.

  • Partner as a farm

    Have surplus? We pick it up for free. Get in touch and we will arrange it.
