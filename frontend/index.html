Give to the pantry

Gifts go into one shared pool, not to a particular box of food. Staff log what the pantry did and what it spent, and that log is what this page shows.

If you give, your own page shows the logged facts and an estimate of your share: your gifts divided by all gifts, applied to what was logged. Estimates are rounded down.
