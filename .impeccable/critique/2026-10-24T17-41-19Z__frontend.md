## Overall Impression
The honest numbers are genuinely distinctive; everything wrapped around them is a template. Biggest opportunity: make the pantry's own words and logs the visual material, and let the donor page read as a letter.

## What's Working
1. The honesty system is the product's identity and is rendered in text, not colour alone.
2. Real state coverage (loading, error, unrecognized, thin, empty) with kind copy and working retry; one dataset and one allocate() keep pages consistent.
3. Admin is action-first: a thank-you queue, records-only drafts, an accessible drawer with focus return and Escape.
