# Cinderwell, sample C: Daylight Signage

- Concept: wayfinding for neighbors first. A clay flood holds the huge uppercase heading, a signal-yellow hours board, and a pictogram row (door, bag, clock; one stroke weight, inline SVG). Thick ink rules, square corners, no shadows. Atkinson 700 for display, Besley italic only for the volunteer quote.
- First mobile screen (390): heading, all three days of hours, and the door/bag/holiday notes. Address is in the door note; the address and a tel link repeat in "Where we are".
- Donor state: the hero slot becomes a slim yellow strip (greeting heading, first sentence of the story, one `<details>` toggle). Records ("Logged by the pantry", solid rule) and estimates ("About your share, an estimate", dashed rule) sit inside the toggle, collapsed by default. Strip is about 190px at 390 including the toggle.
- Host-side enhancement in `site.js`: moves the layer's existing nodes into the `<details>`, splits the story after its first sentence (the rest moves into the toggle, nothing dropped), adds the two group labels, and gives the greeting `role="heading"`. Without JS the slots still read correctly, just expanded. The real layer would need to emit this markup, or the host keeps this script.
- Critique fixes: `.ct-next-link` set at host specificity (ink on yellow, about 12:1, 52px tall); `.ct-record`/`.ct-estimate` weight and italic left to the layer so records stay solid and estimates dashed italic with "about"; slots align to the 1120px column and `.ct-figure` margin is 0; greeting is a real heading; photo hidden under 480px and capped at 10rem otherwise; `.ct-note` is block; tap targets 44px or more (nav, summary, tel link, buttons); display headings line-height 1.2 or more.
- Other changes vs the original: removed the head `<style>` that forced `strong`/`h2` to 2rem; "Find food" now points to the on-page `#find` section (no `visit.html` in this folder); the `localhost:5174` layer script tag is omitted (no external scripts). Copy and figures (hours, address, $35 itemization, "estimate" note) are unchanged.
- One motion: the signal rule under the flood draws in on load; off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Focus rings are 4px (yellow on dark bands), selection/caret/scrollbar/accent-color themed.
- Verified (Browser pane, 1280/768/390, plain and `?preview=1`): no horizontal overflow, `.ct-next-link` computed color rgb(27,17,13) on rgb(246,201,69), no sub-44px targets after the tel-link fix. Not verified: console output (not read), 1280 desktop screenshots were rendered small by the pane, so desktop visuals were checked by layout metrics and a scaled look only; not tested in a real keyboard-only pass.
