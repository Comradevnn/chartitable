Eight volunteers and a borrowed room.

Pellam Street Pantry started in March 2026 in a room a neighbor lent us. There is no staff desk. The person at the table is a volunteer.

A visit

Sign the count sheet with the number of people eating. Take a bag. Rice, beans, oil, and oats are the standing items. Produce depends on the Saturday drop, which is why that line says "about."
