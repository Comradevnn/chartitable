What helps to have

  • A bag, if you already own one. We keep a few paper bags when we have them.
  • A sense of how many people are eating. We do not take an ID.
  • About 20 minutes. Saturday mornings are the busiest hour.

A mix from whatever was invoiced that week. In October 2026 that meant rice, beans, oil, oats, and a produce bag. The produce weight on the shelf list is marked "about" because the bags are filled by eye.
