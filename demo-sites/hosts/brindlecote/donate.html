Where your gift goes

In FY2025, 88 cents of every dollar we spent went to food and programs. Gifts help us:

  • Buy and haul produce from regional farms
  • Stock the shelves at our partner pantries
  • Run mobile pantry stops in neighborhoods without a nearby store

Other ways to give

  • By mail: Brindlecote Food Bank, 418 Millrace Road, Tarnwick
  • Workplace giving and matching gifts
  • Gifts of stock or from a donor-advised fund
  • Food donations: Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm at the warehouse
