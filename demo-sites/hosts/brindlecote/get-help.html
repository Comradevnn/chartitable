Find food near you

Our partner pantries and meal sites are open to anyone in the county who needs food. You do not need to prove need or bring paperwork.

Not sure where to go?

Call our food helpline, Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm. We will find the closest open site for you.

(555) 010-0143

What to bring

  • Bags, boxes, or a cart if you have them. We have some to share.
  • The number of people in your household, so we can plan a fair share.
  • A photo ID or a piece of mail with your address, if you have one. It is not required.
  • Anyone picking up for a neighbor can tell us their name and address.

Common questions

Who can get food?

Anyone who lives in or works in Tarnwick County. There are no income checks at our partner sites.

How often can I visit?

Most sites welcome you once a week. Some meal sites are open every weekday.

Do I need to sign up first?

No. Come during open hours. Seniors and families with small children are helped first at some sites.

Can I choose the food I take?

At sites marked "Choose your own food" you shop the shelves. Other sites hand out pre-packed boxes.
